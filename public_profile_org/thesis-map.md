# Thesis map — five claims, their evidence, and the gap that would falsify each

The frame: **steering an AI agent, raising children and mentoring a junior are one
discipline — the gardener's: respect, direction, no micromanagement.** (Leading a
*team* is the untested scale — see the gap below.)
Five theses, each with a claim, its evidence, its result, an honest status, and the
gap that would falsify it. Statuses: *proven* (a result exists) · *supported* (evidence, no
measured outcome) · *held* (direction felt, mechanism not yet pinned).

## The gardener frame — steering a partner that knows more than any individual
- Claim: one discipline governs every collaboration — a model, a junior, a child, a
  peer: unconditional respect, and guidance by *steering the direction* rather than
  micromanaging, because command fails against a partner whose knowledge exceeds any
  individual's.
- Evidence: respectful partnership ·
  AI-era paradigm (gardener role) ·
  mentoring down-chain ·
  core values vs framework
- Result: three settings, two with a recorded outcome — a mentee's own account of the
  most impactful coaching he had received, children raised on the same respect, and
  JanBot, an agent actually built by steering. All three are *influence without
  formal authority*.
- Status: **supported** — strong across the settings; the *identity* of the
  disciplines is an interpretation, not a controlled comparison.
- Gap: **no formal people-management role — leading a *team* is the untested scale**;
  no controlled test that the method transfers; "gardener" is a frame, not a model.

## Spec-first — define "done" with the stakeholder before starting
- Claim: work starts from an agreed definition of "done", written down before
  implementation, because a shared artifact is the one thing a stakeholder and an
  AI agent can both be held to — and the artifact, not the chat, is where the work
  gets corrected.
- Evidence: 20261007-spec-first-evolution · 20261007-spec-first-pitch ·
  20261015-spec-first-artifacts-in-git · 20261015-janbot-value-proposition ·
  20261007-janbot-ai-agent
- Result: the JanBot evaluation pipeline — a *pre-registered* gate whose verdicts
  moved from "undecidable at n=15" to "decidable at n=44" **by measurement**; and
  artifacts-in-git as a working human↔AI collaboration surface.
- Status: **proven** — a measured, reproducible result.
- Gap: not shown to transfer to a team that did not choose it (demonstration, not
  adoption).

## The AI-era paradigm — determinism, information density, a higher entry level
- Claim: the AI era moves the binding constraint from "what the team already knows"
  to "what the model can hold", so languages that buy determinism (TypeScript, Rust,
  Clojure) gain value rather than losing it, and the developer's role shifts from
  writing code to cultivating it.
- Evidence: 20261016-ai-era-paradigm · 20261007-dna-threads ·
  20261007-janbot-ai-agent
- Result: the direction is exercised (an agent built end to end, a corpus rebuilt),
  but no outcome isolates the paradigm's own contribution.
- Status: **held** — felt, not yet fully articulated (the owner's own words).
- Gap: which changes, how they interact, how to measure them.

## Process → confidence
- Claim: confidence is a by-product of process, not a personality trait — making the
  rule explicit and then verifying it is what turns a felt judgement into something
  that can be checked, and that is why the same habit shows up in the corpus, the
  eval gate and the trading ledger.
- Evidence: 20261007-departure-narrative · 20261007-gtd-org-system-design ·
  20261008-remote-work-conviction · 20261008-gtd-trading-descriptive-phase ·
  the cost of no process ·
  process encoded into construction ·
  freeze → read → rebuild → verify
- Result: the corpus, the eval discipline and the trading ledger are one instrument
  (make it explicit → verify it) — but no self-reported confidence measure exists.
- Status: **supported**.
- Gap: confidence is claimed, not measured.

## The meta-thesis — make the implicit explicit, then verify it
- Claim: every durable method in this corpus reduces to one move — turn the implicit
  into an explicit artifact, then verify the artifact — and the corpus itself is the
  worked example.
- Evidence: all of the above; the corpus itself ·
  the trust-anchor line, made explicit ·
  training on a labelled corpus, 2000s
- Result: the artifacts are the result — the corpus, the pre-registered eval gate and
  the trading ledger each turn a felt rule into a written, checkable object.
- Status: **supported** (self-referential — this map is itself an instance).
- Gap: a method claim; its value shows only when a specific decision improves.
